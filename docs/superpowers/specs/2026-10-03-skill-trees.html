FOTF Skill Trees

Draft talent trees for the Friends of the Forest skills system. Click a node to spend a point; tiers open as you spend more in that tree, and purple boxes are pick-one choices. Purple-edged nodes feed another skill. Pet revival is a separate feature everyone has; Taming's Soul Mender makes it cheaper. Every number is a starting value for the in-game preview.

Every level counts: your total level (all 12 skills added up, max 600) raises max health and ParCool stamina, replacing Paragliders' spirit gems. Each step gives +1 heart and +5% stamina, on a gentle curve (steps get a little longer each time): total levels 8, 19, 32, 46, 61, 78, 95, 113, 132, 151, 171, 192, 213, 234, 256 (15 hearts, all of them by total level 256). Resetting a tree refunds its points for XP levels: 1 level per 2 points spent, minimum 5.
