FOTF Weapon Sorter

Toggle the categories each item should count as, then press Confirm. Skill perks use these categories (Sword perks hit swords, Scythe perks hit scythes and so on). Each weapon's own ability text is listed under it. Add magic when its special attack is a spell: that attack then scales with spell power instead of weapon perks, while normal swings keep the other categories.
