- skill_attack:dummy_drills: needs damage dealt to a target dummy (and a Better Combat combo); written as a timed minecraft:damage_dealt stat
- skill_attack:weapon_of_choice: needs a choice of any one of five iron Spartan weapons; written as the iron longsword
- skill_attack:fallen_adventurer: needs discovery of any fallen adventurer; written as observing a hunted_skeleton

- skill_range:throwing_steel: needs a kill with each thrown weapon; written as crafting all three and six zombie kills
- skill_range:odd_coats: needs specific mob variants killed by arrows; written as quest drops from zombie, spider and skeleton
- skill_taming:stray_wolf: needs a tame task; written as the tame-an-animal advancement
- skill_taming:lame_horse: needs a tame task; written as a checkmark after feeding
- skill_taming:critter_census: needs taming a ferret; written as a checkmark
- skill_taming:sled_dogs: needs taming specific wolves; written as bones consumed and a checkmark
- skill_taming:pet_enchanting: needs a check for enchanted collar tags; written as a checkmark
- skill_taming:butterfly_house: needs releasing butterflies in a build; written as a build, a caterpillar jar and a checkmark
- skill_taming:eggs_to_hatch: needs hatching and raising young; written as collecting eggs and a checkmark
- skill_taming:dragonfly_rider: needs taming a dragonfly; written as observe, armour crafted and a checkmark
