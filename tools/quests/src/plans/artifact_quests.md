# Artifact quests: concepts (SPOILERS)

> Quest content. The pack owner plays unspoiled: don't show this file's contents in summaries.

One quest per artifact, designed backwards from what the artifact does. Not every one is standalone: many are a
step inside the main story, a realm questline or a skill path, with that step shaped around the artifact (method: the "Artifact quests" section of
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-boss-progression.md`). Each line: artifact, what it does, the quest concept, and
where it sits. When a quest is written, set `"signature"` to the artifact and tick it here.

## Mining (Miners' Guild: rescues)
- [ ] **Night Vision Goggles** (see in the dark). A rescue in a mine whose torches have all gone out: find the trapped miner by sound and the few glowing clues; the miner's spare goggles are the thanks. Mining path, early.
- [ ] **Digging Claws** (higher mining level, faster digging). A cave-in with no pickaxe at hand: dig a collapsed tunnel open by hand-tools only, against a timed challenge before the air runs out. Mining path, mid.
- [ ] **Lucky Scarf** (extra fortune). An old prospector swears the scarf brings luck; prove it by bringing ore from three famous seams, then win it in a wager. Mining path, mid.
- [ ] **Pickaxe Heater** (auto-smelts ores). The guild's forge has gone cold deep underground; carry fuel and blaze powder down to relight it, and the smith fits your pick with a heater. Mining path, late (after the Nether).
- [ ] **Onion Ring** (mining speed after eating). The guild's cook feeds the rescue crews: gather the ingredients and cook the shift meal, served before the deepest rescue. Mining path or Cooking crossover.

## Range (Hunters' Lodge: the hunt)
- [ ] **Superstitious Hat** (extra looting). The lodge's old hunter only hunts in a lucky hat; track and bring back trophies from three different beasts and he passes it on. Range path, mid.
- [ ] **Golden Hook** (more XP from creatures). A legendary beast encounter whose trophy is hung on the lodge's golden hook; the hook becomes yours. Range path, capstone-adjacent.

## Fishing (Anglers' Society: competitions)
- [ ] **Angler's Hat** (Luck of the Sea, Lure). The prize of the first fishing competition (timed challenge). Fishing path, early.
- [ ] **Snorkel** (breathe underwater). Retrieve a rod dropped at the bottom of a deep lake; the dive is the point. Fishing path, mid.
- [ ] **Flippers** (better swimming). A swimming race between buoys against a rival (timed, reach a place). Fishing path, mid.
- [ ] **Charm of Sinking** (move freely underwater). A sunken wreck where the treasure is on the sea floor; a diver's charm found in the wreck after clearing it. Fishing path or ocean side quest, late.

## Agility (Couriers: deliveries against the clock)
- [ ] **Running Shoes** (sprint speed, step height). The first timed delivery between two villages; the courier lends you their shoes for good. Agility path, early.
- [ ] **Bunny Hoppers** (jump height, no fall damage). A rooftop route through a village with long drops; timed parkour run. Agility path, mid.
- [ ] **Cloud in a Bottle** (double jump). Catch a cloud: a sky-structure climb to bottle one at the top. Agility path, mid-late (with sky villages or soaring structures).
- [ ] **Panic Necklace** (speed after taking damage). A delivery through hostile ground at night: get hit, keep running. Agility path, mid.
- [ ] **Snowshoes** (walk on powder snow, grip on ice). A winter delivery over powder snow fields to a mountain outpost. Agility path, Frostmaw-area act.
- [ ] **Aqua-Dashers** (run on water while sprinting). A delivery across a lake or bay with no boat allowed (timed, reach a place). Agility path, mid.
- [ ] **Helium Flamingo** (swim in the air). A lost pool float drifts up to a sky structure; climbing after it is the quest. Agility or discovery side quest, fun.
- [ ] **Umbrella** (slow fall, small shield). A courier caught in a storm: deliver a parcel down a cliff route in the rain. Agility side quest, early.

## Defense (Village Watch: guarding a village)
- [ ] **Crystal Heart** (more max health). The watch's oldest guard gives it after the village survives its first big raid with you standing at the gate. Defense path, mid.
- [ ] **Cross Necklace** (longer invincibility after being hit). A priest's keepsake, given for protecting a pilgrim (escort-style encounter) to a chapel. Defense path, mid.
- [ ] **Steadfast Spikes** (no knockback). Hold a narrow bridge against ravagers or knockback mobs for a timed challenge. Defense path, late.
- [ ] **Thorn Pendant** (damages attackers). Clear a thorn-choked shrine (cactus or berry thicket) of what lives in it. Defense side quest.
- [ ] **Flame Pendant** (sets attackers alight, fire resistance after). Defend a village's forge from a Nether raid (blazes or a Wildfire encounter). Defense path, Nether act.
- [ ] **Shock Pendant** (lightning protection, strikes attackers). A lightning-rod tower struck again and again; repair it during a thunderstorm. Defense or Magic side quest.
- [ ] **Obsidian Skull** (temporary fire immunity when burned). Cross a lava lake to reach a Nether outpost's captive; given after the rescue. Defense or Nether realm questline.
- [ ] **Chorus Totem** (teleports you away from a fatal hit). The watch's last-resort relic, earned at the end of the Defense path after holding the line in the End. Defense capstone, End act.

## Attack (Bounty Office: monster hunter)
- [ ] **Power Glove** (more damage). An arena ladder's prize: beat three arena fights in a row. Attack path, mid.
- [ ] **Feral Claws** (attack speed). A beast-tamer's bounty: a wild, fast predator encounter. Attack path, early-mid.
- [ ] **Fire Gauntlet** (fire damage on hits). A bounty in the Nether on a fire-themed mini-boss. Attack path, Nether act.
- [ ] **Vampiric Glove** (melee life steal). A vampire-hunter bounty at night (undead encounter in a crypt); the hunter's glove. Attack path, late.
- [ ] **Pocket Piston** (more knockback). A bounty to clear a cliff-top nest by knocking the mobs off it. Attack side quest.

## Farming (Growers' Co-op: harvest festival)
- [ ] **Rooted Boots** (restore hunger walking on grass). A field that won't grow: restore it (bone meal, irrigation, replanting), and the old farmer's boots are yours. Farming path, mid.
- [ ] **Villager Hat** (cheaper trades). Win the festival's market stall contest by selling the best produce. Farming path or Crafting crossover, late.

## Cooking (Tavern Keepers)
- [ ] **Everlasting Beef** (never runs out). The tavern's legend: a dish that never empties; recreate the recipe chain to earn it. Cooking path, capstone-adjacent.
- [ ] **Eternal Steak** (never runs out). The rival tavern's version; win the cook-off between taverns. Cooking path, late.
- [ ] **Novelty Drinking Hat** (drink faster). Brew for the tavern's festival night. Cooking side quest, fun.
- [ ] **Plastic Drinking Hat** (drink faster). Cheaper twin of the above; a discovery quest in a beach or tavern. Discovery, fun.
- [ ] **Antidote Vessel** (shorter negative effects). Cure a poisoned traveller with the right brews (Magic or Cooking crossover). Magic questline or Cooking side quest.

## Taming (Sanctuary Keepers)
- [ ] **Cowboy Hat** (faster mounts). Round up escaped horses from the sanctuary (encounters across a plain). Taming path, mid.
- [ ] **Kitty Slippers** (creepers avoid you). Rescue a stray cat colony from a creeper-infested ruin. Taming path, early-mid.

## Crafting (Crafters' Guild)
- [ ] **Universal Attractor** (pulls items to you). A guild commission to build a sorting room; the master's prototype attractor is the payment. Crafting path, mid.

## Magic (Arcane Order)
- [ ] **Scarf of Invisibility** (invisibility). Sneak into a cultist or evoker hideout unseen to recover a stolen tome. Magic questline, Act II.

## Discovery and side quests
- [ ] **Whoopee Cushion** (flatulence). A prank side quest in a village (find the cushion hidden by a mischievous villager). Discovery, joke.
